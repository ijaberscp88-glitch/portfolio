About

Building supply chains that perform

I am an operations and supply chain leader with more than three decades of experience across food manufacturing, distribution and international freight.

My career began in freight forwarding with Orient Transport / Kuehne + Nagel and grew through logistics leadership roles in Jordan, Saudi Arabia and the UAE. Today I am Senior Head of Operations at United Foods Company (PJSC) in Dubai, where I also lead the supply chain function.
